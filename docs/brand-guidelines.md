## 3. Brand Voice

### Brand Personality

| Trait | Meaning | How to Apply |
|-------|---------|--------------|
| **Analytical** | Data-first, accurate, objective | Lead with numbers, verified ratings, and exact price deltas |
| **Crisp** | Fast, clear, no unnecessary words | Say what happened in one sentence; avoid marketing filler |
| **Confident** | Authoritative on market truth | State verified facts without hedging or fluff |
| **Autonomous** | Always monitoring, zero-lag | Emphasize 24/7 background tracking and instant notifications |

### Prohibited Words

| Avoid | Use Instead | Why |
|-------|-------------|-----|
| Cheap | Cost-effective / Best Value / Price Drop | Protects premium feel of analytics |
| Guess | Neural Analysis / Verified Extraction | Reinforces machine learning precision |
| Revolutionary | Autonomous / 24/7 Continuous | Avoids over-hyped marketing jargon |
| Just a bot | ReviewPulse Intelligence Assistant | Positions product as an analytical tool |

## 4. Logo & Visual Identity

- **Symbol:** Luminescent glowing shopping tote with an embedded ECG heartbeat/frequency pulse.
- **Logotype:** Wordmark set in Manrope Bold with dual-tone treatment (`Review` in high-contrast Pearl, `Pulse` in Neon Lavender).
- **Favicon:** 32x32 and 64x64 neon gradient pulse icon on dark obsidian backdrop.
- **Clear Space:** Minimum clearance equal to the height of the letter 'R' on all four sides.

---

## 5. Multi-Platform Design Expressions

- **Web Dashboard (Vercel):** Dark cyber-luxury glassmorphism, Recharts visual analytics, interactive harvester.
- **Telegram Bot (@reviewpulse_price_bot):** Clean HTML rich messages with 🟢 / 🔴 / 📊 indicators, bold metrics, and direct links.
- **Automation Cron (GitHub Actions):** Hourly automated background checks with transparent git logging.
